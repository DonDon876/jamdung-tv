1. The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we don't collect information that identifies you.
  • The app sends anonymous play counts (for example which decks are played, which cards are guessed or skipped, and how many games are played each day) so the most popular decks show first and we can improve the game. No name, device ID or advertising ID is attached. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy.
  • The phone apps send crash reports if the app crashes or hits an error, so we can fix it. They contain technical details only. You can switch them off in Settings → Privacy.
  • Loudness measuring is off unless you switch it on. It uses the microphone during turns to measure how loud the room gets as a number. No sound is ever recorded, saved or sent.
  • TV mode shows the timer, scores and deck on your own TV (Chromecast, AirPlay or a cable). The game sends that only to your TV, over your own network or cable, never the card being played, and nothing to us. If you use Chromecast, Google's Cast software in the app finds and connects to your TV, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Your teams, settings, custom decks, uploaded pictures and reaction videos stay on your device.
  • The app is free because of ads. Our ad partner, Chartboost, receives device information such as your advertising ID and IP address so it can show and measure ads.
  • You choose whether ads are personalised (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland), and you can opt out of the "sale or sharing" of your data (United States), in Settings → Games → Ad choices.
  • The reaction camera is off unless you switch it on. Videos are made on your phone and are never uploaded by us.
  • We follow Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020, and the laws of the places where our players live.

  • Reaction videos, until you save or share them or the next turn starts (on phones that can make a highlight reel, until the next game starts).
  • Motion sensor readings used for tilt mode. These are used in the moment and are not stored.

4. Advertising

Ads keep Jamdung - Jamaica Charades free. They can appear in three places: a banner on the start screen, a box on the results screen, and a full-screen ad between some turns. Ads never appear while a team is playing. Each ad may come from Chartboost or be one of our own:

  • Chartboost ads are delivered by Chartboost, Inc. Chartboost collects the information listed in section 3 directly from your device under its own privacy policy at chartboost.com.
  • Our own ads (for example, ads for Jamaican businesses we work with) are shown from pictures included in our deck updates. They do not track you; we only count, anonymously, how often each one is shown and tapped (see section 5a). If you tap one, it opens the advertiser's website or app, which has its own privacy policy.

Personalised ads use information about your device and activity to show ads that match your interests. Non-personalised ads are chosen using limited information such as the app you are in and your general location (country or region), and are still measured for performance and fraud prevention.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

5. Reaction camera

The reaction camera is off by default. If you switch it on, the app asks for permission to use the camera and microphone and films each turn with the front camera so you can watch, save or share the moment.

  • Recording and processing happen entirely on your device. We never receive, upload or store your videos.
  • A video is only saved to your gallery if you tap Save video, and only shared if you tap Share and choose an app such as TikTok, Instagram or WhatsApp. Once shared, that app's privacy policy applies.
  • On phones that support it, the app can make a highlight reel at the end of a game from that game's turn videos, using when cards were guessed and how loud the room was. This happens on your device. The loudness readings for it are kept only in memory and deleted with the videos.
  • Please get the agreement of everyone in the room before filming, especially children.
  • You can switch the camera off in Rules, or remove the permission in your device settings, at any time.

5a. Anonymous play stats and loudness

  • Play stats are on by default. The app adds to running totals of: how often each deck is picked and each card is guessed, skipped or left on screen when time ran out, and how many seconds it was on screen; how many times the app is opened and games, turns and cards are played each day (using your phone's own date and clock, including the hour of day and day of week); which game settings are chosen (tilt or tap, turn length, rounds, solo or teams, number of teams, mixed decks, skip penalty); whether games are played to the end; whether the app runs on Android, iPhone or the web and which decks version it has; how often the filter buttons are tapped; how often our own ads are shown and tapped; and how often a reaction video is filmed, saved or shared (never the video itself). Only these totals are sent, to a database we run on Google Firebase. They carry no name, account, device ID, advertising ID or location, and they are combined with everyone else's, so no one, including us, can tell who played what. Counts made while offline wait on your device and are sent later. Switch them off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous play stats; any counts not yet sent are deleted.
  • Loudness measuring is off by default. If you switch it on, the app asks for microphone permission. During each turn it reads the sound level and turns it into a single number from 0 to 100, and the loudest moment while each card is on screen is added to the play stats. No audio is recorded, stored or sent, and the microphone is closed as soon as the turn ends. Switch it off in Settings → Privacy, or remove the microphone permission in your device settings.
  • The app also downloads the deck totals so it can show the most played decks first under All.

5b. Crash reports

The Android and iPhone apps use Google Firebase Crashlytics. If the app crashes, or an error happens inside the game, a report is sent the next time the app opens, containing the details listed in section 3. Crashlytics links reports from the same installation using a random ID that Firebase creates; it is not your advertising ID and is not used for ads. Crash reports are on by default because they help keep the game working; switch them off in Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days. See Firebase privacy information.

6. Other services the app connects to

When the app opens with an internet connection, it checks for new decks, deck pictures and our own ads in a file hosted by GitHub, Inc., and sends and downloads the anonymous play stats described in section 5a. These requests reveal your IP address and basic device information to those companies, as with visiting any website. We do not receive or keep these details. The app's fonts are built in, so no font service is contacted.

7. Purchases

If we offer an in-app purchase (for example, unlocking extra decks or removing ads), payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or bank details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it, and so you can restore it later.

9. Who data is shared with

We do not sell personal data for money. Information reaches the following only as described above: Chartboost (advertising), GitHub (deck updates), Google (Firebase play stats, Google Play) and Apple (App Store). We may also disclose information if required by Jamaican law or a court order, or to protect the safety of our players.

10. Data sent outside Jamaica

Chartboost, GitHub, Google and Apple are based in the United States and process data in other countries too. When information goes to them, it leaves Jamaica (and, for players elsewhere, their home country). These companies use recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, as required by the Data Protection Act, 2020 and the GDPR, to protect data transferred internationally.

11. How long data is kept

  • Information on your device: until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Reaction videos you did not save: discarded when the next turn starts or the app closes.
  • Crash reports: 90 days, then deleted by Google.
  • Anonymous play stats: kept as running totals for as long as the game is offered. They contain no personal data, so there is nothing to tie back to a player.
  • Information held by Chartboost, GitHub, Google or Apple: for the periods set out in their own privacy policies.

12. Security

The app keeps your information in its private storage on your device, connects to other services over encrypted HTTPS connections, and only asks for the device permissions it needs (internet, vibration, and camera and microphone if you use the reaction camera). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and choose partners with strong security practices.

14. Your choices in the app

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any Chartboost ads load, you choose "Personalised ads" or "Non-personalised ads". Change this at any time in Settings → Games → Ad choices.

16. Children

Jamdung - Jamaica Charades is a party game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. When the app's kids-safe setting is on, Chartboost is told the audience may include children, so ads are never personalised and are handled under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.
